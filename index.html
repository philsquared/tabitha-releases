Tabitha

Every Safari and Chrome tab, across all windows and spaces, one keystroke away.

Download for Mac

Version 0.3.2 · Free · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon · Release notes

Find any tab

From anywhere

Press ⌃⌥T in any app and every open tab (Safari or Chrome) is in front of you.

Every window, every space

Tabs from all your Safari and Chrome windows, in one place. Choosing one takes you to its space.

Type to search

Start typing to narrow the list by title or address, then press return.

See your tabs

Browse as a list, with a large preview, or as a grid of thumbnails.

Keys

⌃⌥TShow or hide Tabitha
↑ ↓Move the selection
↩Switch to the tab
⇥Change view: list, preview or grid
⌘GChange grouping
⌘,Settings
escClose

Private by design

Stays on your Mac

Tabitha never connects to the internet. Your tabs aren't sent anywhere.

Previews in memory only

Tab pictures are never saved to disk, and they're gone when you quit.

Extras are opt-in

Previews and faster switching need extra permissions, so they stay off until you turn them on in Settings.

Install

  1. Open Tabitha.dmg and drag Tabitha to Applications.
  2. Open Tabitha. It lives in the menu bar, not the Dock.
  3. Press ⌃⌥T. macOS asks once whether Tabitha may read your Safari and Chrome tabs.
  4. For tab previews, open Settings and turn on the optional features you want.
